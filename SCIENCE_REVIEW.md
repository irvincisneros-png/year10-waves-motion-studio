# Science Review

## Constants
- Speed of sound in dry air at 20°C: ~343 m/s
- Speed of light in a vacuum: ~3.00 x 10^8 m/s
- Gravity (Earth): ~9.8 m/s^2 (for reference, though mainly using 1D kinematics)

## Assumptions & Simplifications
- Mechanical waves are modelled in an idealised, lossless medium unless friction/damping is explicitly enabled.
- Sound is treated primarily in 1D/2D scalar pressure models.
- Ray optics assume paraxial approximation and thin lenses where applicable.
- Newtonian motion assumes point masses and constant acceleration unless otherwise specified.
- The EM spectrum boundaries are approximations.
