## Model boundary and remaining approval

The simulator is an ideal 1D rope/spring comparison. Real water-surface waves
are not perfectly transverse because particles follow near-circular paths. The
longitudinal display scale protects readable ordering but does not change the
shared engine displacement, phase or feature positions. Builder review does not
replace Irvin's classroom/product approval, science reviewer signoff, real
assistive-technology use or physical-device QA.
