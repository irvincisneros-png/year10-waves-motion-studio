## Scientific and interaction evidence

The React-free engine keeps particle equilibrium positions immutable, calculates
their local displacement from a travelling pulse, and reports zero final net
displacement after the event. Pulse amplitude and width affect local displacement
or duration without changing the fixed propagation speed. Challenge scoring is
derived from captured engine states rather than elapsed clicks or answer text.

Named unit invariants cover control normalisation, propagation, peak displacement,
return home, receiver energy, amplitude/width independence, evidence gating,
reset and deterministic replay. The focused unit gate passed **15 / 15** tests
across the engine and outcome launcher suites.

The focused Chromium + WebKit browser gate passed **18 / 18** tests covering cold
load, one-pulse completion, control causality, state-gated scoring, replay,
reduced motion, Canvas denial, keyboard use, 200% reflow and 320 px touch targets.
