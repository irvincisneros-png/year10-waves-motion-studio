## Known limitations and approval gate

1. The remaining 23 active outcomes are transitional Existing model links. PV-03,
   PV-04 and PV-05 therefore remain deliberately partial at whole-product level.
2. The decorative image has a 199 KB JPEG fallback only; AVIF/WebP conversion was
   unavailable in the local toolchain.
3. Builder review cannot replace Irvin's product/art-direction approval, a science
   reviewer signoff, real assistive-technology use or physical-device/browser QA.
4. The legacy course/workspace routes still exist for non-destructive migration
   and regression safety, but they are absent from primary learner navigation.

Approval of this prototype is the decision gate before any outcome-by-outcome
canonical-route conversion resumes.
