## Removed or intentionally absent

- `public/icons.svg` was an unused third-party social-icon sprite inherited
  from the prototype. It was removed before release; it had no runtime use or
  documented provenance.
- The inherited prototype favicon was replaced by the original WaveMotion
  Studio wave mark above.
- No photographs, stock illustration, AI-generated raster imagery, external
  fonts, patient ultrasound, recorded voices, stellar photographs or cultural
  instrument images ship in the release.
- Outcome 7.2.5 has no asset, route, navigation item or generic substitute.
  Attributable, community-approved sources and assets must be supplied and
  reviewed by Irvin before that outcome can be considered.
